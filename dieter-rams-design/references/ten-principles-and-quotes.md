## De tien principes van goed design

Ontstaan uit de vraag die Rams zichzelf in de jaren zeventig stelde: "Is my design a good design?"
De formulering hieronder is die van Vitsœ, in Rams' eigen woorden.

**1. Good design is innovative**
> "The possibilities for innovation are not, by any means, exhausted. Technological development is always
> offering new opportunities for innovative design. But innovative design always develops in tandem with
> innovative technology, and can never be an end in itself."

**2. Good design makes a product useful**
> "A product is bought to be used. It has to satisfy certain criteria, not only functional, but also
> psychological and aesthetic. Good design emphasizes the usefulness of a product whilst disregarding
> anything that could possibly detract from it."

**3. Good design is aesthetic**
> "The aesthetic quality of a product is integral to its usefulness because products we use every day
> affect our person and our well-being. But only well-executed objects can be beautiful."

**4. Good design makes a product understandable**
> "It clarifies the product's structure. Better still, it can make the product talk. At best, it is
> self-explanatory."

**5. Good design is unobtrusive**
> "Products fulfilling a purpose are like tools. They are neither decorative objects nor works of art.
> Their design should therefore be both neutral and restrained, to leave room for the user's
> self-expression."

**6. Good design is honest**
> "It does not make a product more innovative, powerful or valuable than it really is. It does not
> attempt to manipulate the consumer with promises that cannot be kept."

**7. Good design is long-lasting**
> "It avoids being fashionable and therefore never appears antiquated. Unlike fashionable design, it
> lasts many years – even in today's throwaway society."

**8. Good design is thorough down to the last detail**
> "Nothing must be arbitrary or left to chance. Care and accuracy in the design process show respect
> towards the user."

**9. Good design is environmentally-friendly**
> "Design makes an important contribution to the preservation of the environment. It conserves resources
> and minimizes physical and visual pollution throughout the lifecycle of the product."

**10. Good design is as little design as possible**
> "Less, but better – because it concentrates on the essential aspects, and the products are not burdened
> with non-essentials."

## Quotes per thema

### Minder, maar beter
- "Weniger, aber besser." (Less, but better — zijn levenslange maxime.)
- "Back to purity, back to simplicity. Simplicity is the key to excellence!"
- "Omit the unimportant."
- "Simple is especially hard to achieve."
- "Simple is better than complicated, light is better than heavy and the obvious is better than the
  sought-after."
- "Less but better" — in zijn eigen toelichting: minder producten, maar producten die het leven
  *"facilitate, expand and intensify"*.

### Ethiek: de kardinale zonde
- "Indifference towards people and the reality in which they live is actually the one and only cardinal
  sin in design."
- "A designer who wants to achieve good design must not regard himself as an artist who, according to
  taste and aesthetics, is merely dressing-up products."
- Over wat er moet verdwijnen uit design: "The spectacular. The mendacious."
- Over marketing die zintuigen bespeelt: de "ruthless exploitation of people's weakness for visual and
  haptic signals."

### Visuele vervuiling en gedachteloosheid
- "People of subsequent centuries will get the shivers when they look at the thoughtlessness with which
  we today litter our apartments, our cities, our landscape with a chaos of junk of every description."
  (via Sophie Lovell, *As Little Design as Possible*)
- "If I had to do it over again, I would not want to be a designer. There are too many unnecessary
  products in this world."

### Onopvallendheid
- Apparaten moeten "so restrained and unobtrusive that we hardly notice them unless we need them."
- "Unobtrusive, neutral design is not a shortcoming, but a concrete advantage."
- "Calm, sober and intellectual surprises should always be possible with design."
- "Comfort instead of luxury."

### Duurzaamheid en levensduur
- "Longevity requires the interplay of an intelligent product idea, successful construction, high
  manufacturing quality and well thought-out design."
- "I expect the future to meet the economic and ecological requirements… sustainability in harmony with
  ecology, economy and social responsibility."
- Over veroudering: obsolescence noemde hij "a crime in design".

### Digitaal
- Uit de documentaire *Rams* (Gary Hustwit, 2018): "The world that can be perceived through the senses
  exudes an aura that I believe cannot be digitized. We have to be careful now, that we rule over the
  digital world, and are not ruled by it."
- "The future lies in technology design and interdisciplinarity."
- In de film staat hij in een Apple Store in Londen bij een iPad en betreurt hij dat mensen elkaar niet
  meer aankijken. (Scène, geen citaat — presenteer het niet als quote.)

### Proces en organisatie
- "When you live with products, you get to learn their faults so you can improve them."
- "Good design is a matter for the entire company and not just for individual designers."
- "Design is not an economic factor, but it is economically significant."
- "We need new structures for our behaviours, and that is design."

### Over hem gezegd
- Jonathan Ive: "Dieter Rams' ability to bring form to a product so that it clearly, concisely and
  immediately communicates its meaning is remarkable… He remains utterly alone in producing a body of
  work so consistently beautiful, so right, and so accessible."

## Biografie in het kort

Geboren 20 mei 1932 in Wiesbaden. Opleiding architectuur en binnenhuisarchitectuur aan de Werkkunstschule
Wiesbaden (1947–1953), met een timmermansleertijd ertussen — hij leerde materiaal kennen met zijn handen
voordat hij tekende. In 1955 op 23-jarige leeftijd bij Braun binnengehaald als architect/interieurontwerper;
vanaf 1961 hoofd design, en tot 1997 aan Braun verbonden. Mentoren en collega's: Fritz Eichler, en vanuit
de Hochschule für Gestaltung Ulm Hans Gugelot en Otl Aicher. Sinds 1959 verbonden aan Vitsœ — "Thus the
ideology behind my design is engrained within the company."

Onderscheidingen o.a.: Bundesverdienstkreuz (Commander's Cross, 2002), Lifetime Achievement Medal London
Design Festival (2013), Compasso d'Oro alla Carriera (2014), iF Design Lifetime Achievement Award (2024),
World Design Medal (2025).

## Sleutelprojecten

**Braun SK 4 radio-platenspeler (1956, met Hans Gugelot)** — bijgenaamd "Snow White's coffin" vanwege het
transparante plexiglas deksel. Brak met de gewoonte om apparaten als meubelstuk te vermommen: het ding
is wat het is.

**Braun T3 zakradio (1958)** — het ronde duimwiel en het rooster van gaatjes op een rechthoekig wit vlak;
regelrechte voorouder van de iPod met zijn click wheel.

**Vitsœ 606 Universal Shelving System (1960)** — planksysteem dat meegroeit met een leven en met een huis:
je kunt het uitbreiden, verplaatsen, halveren. Wordt vandaag vrijwel ongewijzigd verkocht; oude en nieuwe
onderdelen passen op elkaar. Zijn sterkste argument voor levensduur boven vervanging.

**Vitsœ 620 Chair Programme (1962)** — fauteuil als bouwsysteem, uit te breiden tot bank, uit elkaar te
nemen voor verhuizing.

**Braun T2 aansteker (1968)** — een cilinder die niets voorwendt; latere versies met piëzo- en
zonnemechaniek.

**Braun ET66 rekenmachine (1987)** — het gele =-toetsje, de ronde toetsen, de rustige raster. Herkenbaar
terug in de rekenmachine-app van de vroege iPhone.

**Braun TG 60 bandrecorder** — de vormtaal die Apple later citeerde in de Podcasts-app.

**Braun 4 Series / audio-apparaten, keukenmachines, scheerapparaten, klokken** — bij elkaar meer dan
duizend producten in vier decennia, allemaal getoetst aan dezelfde vraag: verdient elk element zijn plek?
